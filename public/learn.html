Beginner fundamentals

Start with the core chain

These lessons teach the main monthly flow: growth, inflation, Fed expectations, yields, USD, then Gold/NAS100/BTC reaction.

Terms To Know First

Learn these words first. They make the rest of fundamentals much easier to understand.

The Monthly Order

The order matters because each report prepares the market for the next one. PMI is usually the first clue. NFP confirms whether businesses are hiring. PPI warns about producer inflation. CPI is the major consumer inflation test.

1. PMIForward-looking business activity.
2. NFPLabor market confirmation.
3. PPIWholesale inflation pressure.
4. CPIConsumer inflation and Fed repricing.

Growth: PMI + NFP

PMI and NFP are the growth side of the story. Strong PMI can hint that NFP may be strong because business demand can become hiring demand.

  • PMI above 50 means expansion.
  • PMI below 50 means contraction risk.
  • NFP beat means labor is strong and the Fed has less reason to cut.
  • NFP miss means growth may be slowing and Gold can get support if yields fall.

Inflation: PPI + CPI

PPI comes before CPI because producer costs can move into consumer prices. If PPI is hot, traders often prepare for CPI risk.

Example

Strong PMI + strong NFP + hot PPI means CPI may come hot. That usually means Fed higher-for-longer, yields up, USD up, Gold/NAS100 pressure.

What Are Yields?

Yields are the interest rate investors earn from holding government bonds. In this app, US yields usually mean the US10Y and US2Y Treasury yields.

Higher yieldsBonds pay more interest. USD usually strengthens. Gold and NAS100 usually get pressured.
Lower yieldsBonds pay less interest. USD usually weakens. Gold and NAS100 can rally.
Beginner chain

Hot CPI or strong NFP → Fed may keep rates higher → bond yields rise → USD gets stronger → Gold gets pressured → NAS100 gets pressured.

Fed, Bonds, USD

The Fed path is the bridge between economic data and asset prices. Hot inflation and strong jobs push the market toward hikes or higher-for-longer. Weak data pushes toward cuts.

Higher yieldsStronger USD, Gold bearish, NAS100 bearish.
Lower yieldsWeaker USD, Gold bullish, NAS100 bullish.

Asset Reactions

GoldBearish when USD/yields rise

Gold has no yield, so higher real yields make it less attractive.

NAS100Bearish when rates rise

Tech valuations are sensitive to borrowing costs and discount rates.

US30Depends on growth vs rates

Dow likes growth, but hawkish Fed pressure can still drag it.

BTCRisk-on liquidity asset

BTC likes risk-on and weaker USD/yields.

JPYSafe haven

JPY can strengthen when risk-off fear rises.

Tutorial: How To Trade The News

News trading is not guessing the number. It is preparing scenarios, waiting for the release, and only trading when price confirms the macro story.

Advanced fundamentals

Learn what can override the basic chain

After you understand PMI, NFP, PPI and CPI, study the forces that can change the market reaction: Fed tone, real yields, liquidity, positioning, auctions, oil and risk sentiment.

Advanced Macro Concepts

Advanced macro is about knowing which force is currently controlling the market. Sometimes inflation controls. Sometimes growth controls. Sometimes liquidity or geopolitics overrides everything.

More Fundamental Drivers To Watch

These are not always the main event, but they can move yields, USD, Gold, NAS100, US30 and BTC when the market is sensitive to them.

Conflicting Data Playbooks

The market often gets mixed data. The job is to know which report has priority and what confirmation you need before trading.

Top-Down Trading Framework

1. RegimeGrowth, inflation, Fed, liquidity, risk mood.
2. CatalystToday’s event: CPI, NFP, FOMC, PMI, PPI.
3. ConfirmationDXY, US10Y, yields, market breadth, asset reaction.
4. ExecutionOnly trade when macro story and price reaction agree.
